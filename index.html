Official Onboarding Guide

How to Register as a BoloUp App Host

01

Step 1: Download and Register

Download the BoloUp app and create your account using one of the available sign-up methods.

02

Step 2: Open Your Profile

Go to your profile and find the Become Host section. Tap Join to begin your host application.

03

Step 3: Enter Your Agency ID

Open the Join Agency form and enter the Agency ID provided by the agency you want to join (2525968) . Check the agency information before submitting
